## Tradeoffs and negative outcomes

- Every protected method met its retained-bank contract. That did not prevent all unseen partial-cue failures.
- Full and mixed margin banks behaved like unprotected adaptation here. Their limits did not constrain this task enough to establish a benefit.
- The boundary bank lost fewer individual cue routes, while completing less of the requested objective change. This is a tradeoff at unequal progress, not proof of better protection at matched learning.
- Growth was triggered 0 times. The growing condition completed its task in existing capacity. The larger control adds measurement cost without improving mean contrast accuracy in this receipt. Growth benefit remains unestablished.
- Neither the calibration bank nor the confidence policy sees held-out labels. The experimenter uses those labels to score all outcomes.
